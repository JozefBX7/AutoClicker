# Settings and backups

## Appearance

On its first run, AutoClicker follows your Windows light/dark preference. The title-bar theme button saves your chosen theme. The pin button keeps the window above other apps, and the centre bar switches to a compact view. Both choices are remembered.

## Defaults

**Set as default** saves the current main-window setup, including your interval, action, repeat rule, position, hotkey, and lighting settings.

**Settings → Reset to defaults** restores the original setup: F6, a 100 ms interval, and RGB lighting off.

## Backups

Use **Settings → Configuration backup** to export or import your full setup. A full backup includes your defaults, appearance, lighting settings, custom sequence, and saved presets.

Installed copies keep data in `%LocalAppData%\AutoClicker`. Portable copies keep it in a `Data` folder beside the app. Use the backup controls rather than editing these files yourself.

## Logs

AutoClicker writes `AutoClicker.log` beside the app when possible, or in its data folder. Logs are kept to a sensible size and can help when reporting a problem.
